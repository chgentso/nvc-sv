# of the value (the undefined flag adds a few gates per division and index).

# constant fold away: the divider by a constant is much smaller).  Signedness
# changes only the sign handling of the divider, the comparison and the
# arithmetic shift.  A shift by a constant (5 here) is wiring.
#
